DOCUMENT 2: Onboarding & Training Guidelines
Title: Employee Onboarding & Continuous Professional Development
Summary: Guidelines regarding new hire orientation, mandatory compliance training modules, tuition reimbursement policies, and leadership development programs.
Key Policies:
- Orientation must be completed within the first 30 days of employment.
- Annual learning stipend is set at $1,500 per full-time employee.
- Security and compliance certification renewals are due quarterly.
Keywords: onboarding, training stipend, professional development, orientation, compliance, learning modules.
