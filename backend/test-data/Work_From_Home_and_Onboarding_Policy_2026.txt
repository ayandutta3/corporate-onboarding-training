================================================================================
TCS CORPORATE POLICY 2026: WORK FROM HOME (WFH) AND REMOTE ONBOARDING GUIDELINES
================================================================================

1. ELIGIBILITY AND PERMISSION SCOPE
- All full-time employee roles across HR, Engineering, Finance, and Corporate operations are eligible for 2 days per week Remote / Work From Home (WFH).
- Employees must maintain a minimum 80% quarterly office attendance baseline for the remaining 3 days.

2. MANDATORY APPROVAL STEPS
- Step 1: Submit a WFH request via the Corporate Employee Portal 48 hours prior to the requested remote date.
- Step 2: Line Manager or Department VP must approve the ticket digitally.
- Step 3: Automated IT Security check verifies encrypted VPN registration on the employee laptop.

3. CORE WORKING HOURS AND COMPLIANCE
- Mandatory Core Availability Window: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST across all remote work days.
- Total required daily working hours: 8 hours per day.
- Unscheduled offline status exceeding 45 minutes during core hours requires prior notification to the team lead.

4. IT AND DATA SECURITY COMPLIANCE
- Remote work is permitted ONLY on company-issued laptops with active BitLocker drive encryption and GlobalProtect VPN.
- Public Wi-Fi without VPN encryption is strictly prohibited.
- Sharing confidential onboarding or project documentation on personal cloud storage is a zero-tolerance compliance violation.
