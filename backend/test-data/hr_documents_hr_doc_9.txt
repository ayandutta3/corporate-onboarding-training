DOCUMENT 9: Global Relocation & International Mobility Framework
Title: Expatriate Transfers, Tax Equalization, and Relocation Allowances
Details:
- Relocation Package: Includes temporary housing for up to 60 days, shipping of personal effects up to $10,000, and full tax advisory services.
- Repatriation Support: If an international assignment ends prematurely due to corporate restructuring, full return travel costs are covered by HR Mobility.
- Language & Cultural Training: Up to 40 hours of formal language instruction provided for the employee and legal spouse.
