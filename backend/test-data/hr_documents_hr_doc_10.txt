DOCUMENT 10: Special Termination, Severance & Non-Compete Clauses
Title: Outplacement Services, Exit Benefits, and Severance Schedule
Details:
- Severance Pay: 2 weeks of base salary per year of completed service, capped at 26 weeks.
- Continuation of Benefits: Health coverage (COBRA subsidy) provided for up to 3 months post-separation for non-cause terminations.
- Outplacement Services: Senior staff receive 6 months of dedicated career coaching and resume optimization support upon exit.
