DOCUMENT 4: Employee Leave & Time-Off General Standard
Title: Annual Leave, Sick Leave, and Paid Time Off (PTO) Guidelines
Summary: General overview of standard paid leave allocations, holiday calendars, bereavement leave standards, and PTO request workflows.
Key Policies:
- Full-time staff receive 20 days of base PTO per calendar year.
- Unused PTO rollover is capped at 5 days into the following year.
- Sick leave requires medical documentation after 3 consecutive days.
Keywords: paid time off, PTO rollover, holiday calendar, sick leave, absence policy, personal leave.
