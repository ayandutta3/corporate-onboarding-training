DOCUMENT 7: Special Operations Protocol - Emergency Leave & Sabbatical Addendum
Title: Non-Standard Leave Policies, Extended Sabbaticals, and Emergency Grants
Details:
- Sabbatical Policy: Staff with 5+ consecutive years of service are eligible for up to 12 weeks of partially paid sabbatical (50% base salary).
- Crisis Grant Fund: Employees experiencing acute emergency financial hardship can request a non-repayable grant up to $5,000 from the HR Emergency Fund.
- Extended Parental Leave: Up to 16 weeks fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers, with an optional 4-week flexible return-to-work phase.
