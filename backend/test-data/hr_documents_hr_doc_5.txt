DOCUMENT 5: Performance Management & Promotion Criteria
Title: Career Growth, Evaluation Metrics, and Promotion Pathways
Summary: Standard performance evaluation benchmarks, KPI tracking procedures, promotion timelines, and merit increment criteria.
Key Policies:
- Mid-year check-ins occur in June; formal evaluations take place in December.
- Promotions require a minimum of 12 months in current role and 'Exceeds Expectations' rating.
- Merit increases are tied directly to annual evaluation ratings.
Keywords: career growth, performance rating, promotion criteria, merit increment, KPI, evaluation.
