================================================================================
TCS GLOBAL ENTERPRISE LEAVE POLICY AND TIME-OFF GUIDELINES (2026 REVISION)
================================================================================

CONFIDENTIALITY NOTICE AND STANDARD HR DISCLAIMER:
This document contains official corporate leave policy guidelines for all full-time employees, contractors, and business unit leaders across HR, Finance, Engineering, and Corporate Operations. Unauthorized external distribution is strictly prohibited. All employees must review annual leave allowances prior to submitting time-off requests in the Corporate HR Portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: CASUAL LEAVE (CL) DIRECTIVES AND LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Entitlement and Annual Allowance:
All regular full-time employees are entitled to 7 days of Casual Leave (CL) per calendar year (1st January to 31st December). Casual leave is intended for unexpected personal matters, short family obligations, and urgent personal tasks. It cannot be combined with long vacation leaves.

1.2 Monthly Capping and Minimum Units:
- Employees may take a maximum of 3 days of Casual Leave in any single calendar month.
- Minimum allowable unit for Casual Leave is 0.5 days (half-day).
- Maximum continuous duration for Casual Leave is 3 days at a single stretch.
- Any continuous absence exceeding 3 consecutive days must be requested as Earned Leave (Privilege Leave) or Leave Without Pay.

1.3 Emergency Application Procedure:
Prior digital permission via the Corporate Portal is required at least 24 hours in advance. In case of unexpected emergencies, verbal or email notification must be sent to the immediate Line Manager before 10:00 AM EST on the day of absence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE (SL) AND MEDICAL CERTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Annual Entitlement:
All employees are entitled to 7 days of paid Sick Leave per calendar year. Sick leave is allocated for personal medical illness, injury, or quarantine directives issued by registered medical authorities.

2.2 Medical Certificate Requirements:
- For any sick leave absence exceeding 2 consecutive working days, a signed medical certificate from a licensed Medical Practitioner must be submitted upon resuming duty.
- Failure to provide valid medical documentation for absences longer than 2 days will result in the leave being categorized as Unauthorized Leave Without Pay (LWP).

2.3 Non-Encashable and Non-Accumulative Rule:
Sick leave cannot be carried forward to subsequent calendar years. Any unutilized sick leave at the end of December 31st will lapse automatically without financial encashment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: EARNED LEAVE (PRIVILEGE LEAVE - PL) AND CARRY FORWARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Accrual Rate:
Earned Leave accrues at the rate of 1 day for every 20 completed working days. Employees must complete a minimum of 240 actual working days in a calendar year to qualify for full Earned Leave accrual.

3.2 Long Vacation Advance Notice:
- Earned Leave is designed for planned extended vacations, family trips, and personal sabbaticals.
- Formal digital submission via the Corporate HR Portal must be made at least 7 working days prior to the proposed leave start date.
- Employees are permitted a maximum of 3 Earned Leave installments per calendar year.

3.3 Carry Forward and Maximum Accumulation Limit:
- Unused Earned Leave may be carried forward to subsequent calendar years up to a maximum limit of 45 days.
- Any accrued Earned Leave balance exceeding 45 days at year-end will automatically lapse unless explicit approval for encashment is granted by Executive Management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: LEAVE ENCASHMENT FORMULA AND TAXATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Encashment Formula:
Eligible employees may request financial encashment of accumulated Earned Leave during active service or upon separation. The calculation formula is:
Encashment Value = (Monthly Gross Salary / 26) * Number of Encashable Leave Days

4.2 Tax Compliance:
- Leave encashment received during active service is fully taxable under standard employment income tax brackets.
- Leave encashment received upon retirement or formal resignation is subject to statutory tax exemption caps as per prevailing tax regulations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5: PATERNITY, MATERNITY, AND SPECIAL LEAVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Maternity Leave:
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid Maternity Leave in accordance with the Statutory Maternity Benefit Act. Application must be submitted 6 weeks prior to expected delivery.

5.2 Paternity Leave:
Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid Paternity Leave upon the birth or legal adoption of a child, to be utilized within 6 months of delivery.

5.3 Bereavement Leave:
Employees are granted up to 3 days of paid Bereavement Leave in the unfortunate event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
